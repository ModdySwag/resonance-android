Use: headphones for binaural beats — the beat is created in your hearing, not in the file; isochronic pulse presets and the whole Speakers pack work on loudspeakers, and nothing in that pack depends on a difference between your ears. Care: keep volumes moderate — a pulse train is more alerting than a steady tone at the same level, and if you have to raise your voice to be heard at arm's length it is too loud. If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, ask a clinician before using repetitive pulsed tones. The visualizer panels can be switched off entirely (V, or the Visualizers card) and are capped well below the three-flashes-per-second accessibility threshold, but skip them too if flashing images affect you. Not a medical device or treatment; skip it while driving or operating machinery, and stop if it feels unpleasant.
