## Full description — limit 4000

<!--full-->
Resonance Studio is a tone, beat and noise instrument that runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is streamed, nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to make. Every sound is synthesised on the device as you hear it.

It is a real studio underneath the simple face: a multi-voice schedule engine, 100+ built-in sessions, an editor, and a visualiser that shows what the tone actually does to a plate, a water surface or a cloud of bubbles. You can start listening in one tap from the home screen, or open the studio and shape every layer.

WHAT IT DOES

• Sleep, Focus, Relax and Wake tiles start a ready-made session in one tap
• Build a tone: pick a carrier, a beat rate and an optional noise bed, then press play
• 100+ sessions across the binaural, monaural, isochronic and noise families, including a whole speaker-friendly pack for people without headphones
• Every session is editable — voices, ramps, durations and levels are not locked
• A Visualise section that binds a cymatic chamber to the session: the carrier picks the plate mode, the beat sets the pace, and the medium follows the voice type
• Live meters, a session timer, a breathing pacer and an instant mute
• Session timer stops the sound for you; the notification and lock screen keep pause and stop
• Export the schedule as .gnaural or render it to a WAV file of any length
• Save packs and export straight to Downloads/Resonance Studio

HONEST ABOUT HEADPHONES

A binaural beat is made in your hearing, not in the file, so binaural sessions need headphones. The Speakers pack and the isochronic pulses work on any loudspeaker and depend on nothing about your ears. The app says which one you are holding.

THE VISUALISER

The chamber is not decoration. It solves the vibration modes of a real free plate, a bolted plate, a disc, a drum, a water surface and an air-bubble cloud, then shows the figure the current tone drives. Slow delta settles sand on a big plate; fast beta ripples water; a noise bed shakes a skin. Everything is capped below the three-flashes-per-second threshold and can be switched off.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

No analytics, no ads, no login, no upload. The whole studio — engine, sessions, editor, packs and visualisers — ships inside the app, so it works in a tunnel. The only network use is the update check and the help pages, which open in your browser.

ACCESSIBLE

Material 3 surfaces, a 48 dp minimum on every control, screen-reader labels on every icon button, reduced-motion support, a light and a dark theme, and an accent that can follow your wallpaper.

A NOTE ON CLAIMS

Binaural and isochronic tones are entertainment and a nudge, not a treatment: the defensible claim is that a periodic tone produces a measurable neural response locked to its rate while it plays, not that it changes your brain for good. If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, ask a clinician before using repetitive pulsed tones.

Resonance Studio is free, open source, and has no locked features.
