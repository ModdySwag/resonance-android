Privacy

Resonance Studio for Android collects nothing, uploads nothing, and has no account. This page says exactly what that means.

What is stored, and where

All of it stays on your phone, inside the app's own storage:

  • your preferences — theme, accent, volume, loop, the visualiser's settings;
  • your favourites;
  • packs you import;
  • files you explicitly save, which go to Downloads/Resonance Studio where any file manager can see them.

Nothing above is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all of it except the files you saved, which you delete like any other download.

What goes over the network

  • The update check. The app fetches a small JSON file from moddys.net to see whether a newer version exists. It sends no identifier, no cookie and no account — the request is the same one any browser makes for a file.
  • Help and credits. Those pages open in your browser, so they are an ordinary web visit under the site's own terms.

Nothing else touches the network. The engine, the sessions, the editor, the packs and the visualisers all ship inside the app and work in a tunnel.

What is never collected

  • no analytics and no crash reporting SDK;
  • no advertising and no ad identifier;
  • no location, contacts, calendar, photos or microphone access;
  • no account, email or profile.

The app has no third-party dependencies at all: it is the platform WebView plus its own Java shell.

Permissions and why

Internet (update check, help pages) · network state (skip the check on a dead link) · wake lock and a media foreground service (keep playing with the screen off, with pause and stop on the lock screen) · notifications (the playback notification) · install packages (the app updates itself; you still confirm) · write external storage, only on Android 9 and older (save to Downloads).

Claims

Tones here are entertainment and a nudge, not a medical device or treatment. If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, ask a clinician before using repetitive pulsed tones. Keep volumes moderate. See the help pages for the details.
